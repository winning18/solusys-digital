Sales & Business Development Executive

Join Solusys Digital and turn conversations into opportunities.

Apply Now Read the Full Role

Are you a university student, fresh graduate, or young professional with excellent English communication skills and the confidence to make cold calls?

Solusys Digital is looking for a motivated Sales & Business Development Executive to help us identify businesses and organizations that need digital solutions and introduce them to how we can help solve their problems.

// WHAT YOU'LL DO
  • Identify and research potential clients who may need our services
  • Make cold calls and initiate conversations with prospects
  • Understand a prospect's business needs and challenges
  • Explain how Solusys Digital's products and services can help
  • Follow up with prospects and maintain relationships
  • Schedule meetings and help move qualified prospects toward becoming clients
  • Keep accurate records of leads and sales activities
// WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR
  • University students, fresh graduates, or entry-level applicants are welcome
  • Excellent spoken and written English
  • Confident and comfortable speaking with people over the phone
  • Ability to make cold calls without hesitation
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills
  • Proactive, ambitious, and willing to learn
  • Interest in technology, digital solutions, and business is an advantage
  • Ability to operate a computer
  • Previous sales experience is not required if you can demonstrate confidence, communication ability, and a willingness to learn
// WHY JOIN SOLUSYS DIGITAL

This is an opportunity to gain practical experience in sales, business development, technology, and client acquisition while working with a growing digital solutions company.

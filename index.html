// FAQ

Common questions.

What services does Solusys Digital offer?

Graphic design, web and app development, data annotation, AI automation, CCTV installation, home and office electricals, security fencing, and home automation. You can combine any of these into a single project.

Do you work with clients outside Accra?

Yes. Digital services like design, development, and AI automation can be delivered remotely to clients anywhere. Installation based services such as CCTV, electricals, and fencing are currently offered in and around Accra. Reach out and we'll confirm for your location.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form on our Contact page with a bit of detail about your project, or message us directly on WhatsApp. We'll get back to you with next steps.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the service and scope. Design and web projects can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks depending on complexity, while installation projects are usually scheduled shortly after an on-site assessment. We'll give you a clear timeline once we understand your project.

Do you offer support after a project is completed?

Yes. We provide ongoing maintenance and support for websites and apps after launch, and maintenance and upgrades for installed systems like CCTV, electricals, and fencing.

Can you handle more than one service in the same project?

Yes, that's one of the reasons Solusys Digital exists. It's common for a single project to combine, for example, a website build with CCTV installation, or AI automation with data annotation. One team handles the whole thing.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're trying to build, automate, or secure, and we'll tell you exactly how we'd approach it.
